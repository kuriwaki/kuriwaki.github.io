About the banner image: President-House ticket splitting by district. Survey data from the Cumulative CES for the 2008–2025 presidential elections, limited to major-party presidential and House voters in contested districts. District estimates use a hierarchical model fitted with lme4, with predictors including House candidate's incumbency (CAGE), tenure, party, the district's presidential voteshare (ccesMRPprep), district, region, time trends, and alignment with the sitting president's party. Colors show estimated ticket splitting as a share of the district electorate. Percentages above the maps show the national estimated President-House ticket splitting rate. Districts without competing major-party candidates are blank. Figure created by Shiro Kuriwaki with assistance by Zoya Ahmer.
