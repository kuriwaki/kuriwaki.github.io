For the fall 2026 semester, I’m working with Professor Shiro Kuriwaki as a pedagogical partner for Applied Quantitative Research Design through Yale’s [Poorvu Center for Teaching and Learning](https://poorvucenter.yale.edu/teaching/teaching-programs-and-events/pedagogical-partnerships). My role is to help the teaching team understand how students experience the course. I observe classes, take notes on teaching and student engagement, and discuss my observations with the teaching team. I also collect student feedback to understand what supports or hinders their learning.

In this blog, I will share reflections from our class as we grapple with how AI tools are reshaping student learning and how effective cold calling is for encouraging participation and gauging understanding. I hope these reflections offer other instructors useful questions to ask about their own courses.
